### Warp Drive Theories and Models

#### Alcubierre Metric (1994)
The Alcubierre metric describes a spacetime geometry where a bubble of flat space moves faster than light by contracting space ahead and expanding it behind. This requires negative energy density, violating classical energy conditions with total energy equivalent to the mass of Jupiter \[1\].

#### Natário Drive (2002)
The Natário drive is an alternative metric that uses zero expansion, creating a vector field that slides space like a conveyor belt. It avoids volume changes and blue-shifting radiation at the front of the bubble but still requires exotic matter for FTL travel \[2\].

#### Lentz Solitons (2021)
Erik Lentz published "Hyper-fast travel within general relativity" showing that a class of soliton solutions to Einstein field equations can achieve superluminal travel using only positive energy densities. These hyper-fast solitons are shell-like topologies of space-time that move at arbitrary velocities while containing a flat interior, eliminating the need for exotic matter \[3\].

### Theoretical Frameworks and Feasibility Studies

#### Bobrick-Martire Framework (2021)
Bobrick and Martire published "Introducing physical warp drives" creating a general classification framework for all warp drive spacetimes. They decomposed them into: the shell (modified spacetime), the interior (passenger volume), and the exterior (flat space). They proved that subluminal positive-energy warp drives are physically valid without exotic matter below light speed \[4\].
